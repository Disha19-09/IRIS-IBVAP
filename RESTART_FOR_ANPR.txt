The ANPR (plate recognition) feature has been fixed!

The system was running WITHOUT the --anpr flag, so plates were
not being recognized. This has now been corrected.

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  Quick Restart Instructions                                    │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

1. Double-click:  STOP_ALL.bat
   (Stops all running services)

2. Wait 5 seconds

3. Double-click:  START.bat
   (Starts everything WITH ANPR enabled)

4. Wait 30 seconds for startup

5. Open browser: http://127.0.0.1:5173/
   
6. Go to ANPR tab and watch for plate detections!

┌───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  What to Expect                                                │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

When a vehicle crosses the virtual fence:
  ✓ Plate number will be recognized by OCR
  ✓ Plate displayed on video overlay (yellow text)
  ✓ Plate recorded in database
  ✓ Plate visible in ANPR tab of dashboard
  ✓ Terminal shows: "[ALERT] car #123 | Plate: ABC1234"

First time only:
  - EasyOCR will download language models (~50MB)
  - Takes about 1 minute
  - Subsequent runs are instant
