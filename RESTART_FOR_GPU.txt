│  Troubleshooting                                               │
└───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘

If you see "CUDA not available":
  1. Check: nvidia-smi
  2. Reinstall PyTorch with CUDA:
     pip install torch torchvision --index-url 
     https://download.pytorch.org/whl/cu118

If "Out of memory":
  • Reduce RESIZE_WIDTH to 960 in detect_track_fence.py
  • Or change stream to 1280:720 in START.bat
